Our programs are designed for civil engineering students, fresh graduates, aspiring structural detailers, estimators and working professionals looking to strengthen their practical skills.
Prior professional experience is not necessarily required. The learning path is structured to introduce fundamental concepts before progressing into practical structural workflows.
The program covers structural drawing interpretation, terminology, structural members, reinforcement placement, bar marks, structural elements, drawing preparation and practical project exercises.
Practical assignments and project-oriented exercises are an important part of the learning approach, helping learners apply concepts to structural engineering workflows.
The 3D Rebar Detailing program is currently planned as an advanced future program and will be introduced as part of APT Structural Campus's next-generation learning pathway.
